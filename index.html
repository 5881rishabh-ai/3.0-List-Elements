Rohan's Masala Chai Recipe

Ingredients

For the tea base

  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cup full cream milk
  • 2 teaspoons loose black tea leaves
  • 2 tablespoons sugar (or to taste)
  • 1 small piece of ginger (crushed)
  • 4-5 green cardamom pods

For the spice mix:

  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 3-4 cloves
  • 1/2 teaspoon black peppercorns

Instructions

  1. Pour water into a saucepan and add the crushed ginger and whole spices.
  2. Bring the water to a boil and let the spices simmer for 2-3 minutes.
  3. Add the black tea leaves and boil for another 1-2 minutes.
  4. Pour in the milk and add sugar.
  5. Bring the mixture to a boil again, then reduce heat and simmer for 2 minutes.
  6. Strain the tea into cups using a fine sieve.
  7. Serve hot and enjoy with biscuits or pakoras.
